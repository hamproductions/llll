## Product Direction

The user does **not** want a raw asset archive UI.

The app should represent the game and its user-facing entities:

- Home
- Cards
- Stories
- Songs
- Characters
- Gacha
- Stickers
- Downloads
- BGM
- Grand Prix
- Items
- Emoji
- Viewer / future Spine Viewer

Primary rule:

- list routes should stay light and browsable
- heavy detail belongs on `/<entity>/<id>`
- avoid dev-facing filenames, IDs, and asset jargon in the visible UI unless strictly necessary

The user explicitly hates:

- raw archive framing
- tables for user-facing content
- overstuffed list pages
- top bar clutter
- aggressive image cropping
- showing too much metadata in list cards
- developer-y labels / file names / ID badges everywhere

## Story Asset Families

This was a major source of confusion and is now clarified.

There are three distinct story image families relevant here:

1. `story_thumbnail_*`
   - small/square thumbnail family
   - used as supporting thumbnail imagery

2. `image_record_monthly_*`
   - series/chapter cover family
   - keyed by series-like IDs such as `105012`
   - bundle count observed: `44`

3. `image_record_monthly_part_*`
   - per-part image family
   - keyed by part/script IDs such as `10501201`
   - bundle count observed: `393`

Correct usage:

- story list card: `image_record_monthly_<seriesId>`
- story detail hero for a series/chapter: `image_record_monthly_<seriesId>`
- story part rows: `image_record_monthly_part_<scriptId>`
- thumbnail accent/supplement: `story_thumbnail_<scriptId>`
