# Cards

The cards section presents game cards in a browsable, visually respectful way.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Card list page is light and compact, not dense or overstuffed
- Card images use `contain` fitting, not `cover` — no aggressive cropping
- Minimal metadata on the list page; heavy detail belongs on `/cards/:id`
- Card detail page shows full card art, stats, and related media
- No developer-facing IDs, filenames, or asset jargon visible in the UI
