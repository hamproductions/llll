# Emoji

An emoji page backed by the emojis.tsv and emojicategory.tsv data files.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Emoji page exists at `/emoji`
- Emojis are loaded from TSV data with category grouping
- Categories from emojicategory.tsv are used for organization
- Display name, category, and unlock condition metadata are shown
- The page is browsable even before atlas/sprite extraction is complete
- Placeholder or metadata-only rendering is acceptable until real emoji images are extracted
